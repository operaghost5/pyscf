# CNEO-DFT hydrogen-bonded dimers: project summary

Status of the work done in this project up to 1 November 2026. Everything
listed as committed is on branch `nqe-dimer-inputs` of `operaghost5/pyscf`,
folder `nqe_dimer_inputs/`; each set's README and `METHODS.md` there hold the
full detail.

## 1. Scope

Nuclear quantum effects in hydrogen-bonded and halogen-bonded dimers,
treated with CNEO-DFT (`pyscf.neo.CDFT` in the theorychemyang/pyscf fork)
and compared with conventional DFT on the same footing.

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Functionals | PBE, PW91, BP86, BLYP, B97 (hybrid, `HYB_GGA_XC_B97`) |
| Electronic bases | aug-cc-pVDZ, aug-cc-pVTZ, aug-cc-pVQZ (aug-cc-pV5Z excluded throughout) |
| Protonic basis | PB4-D; no electron–proton correlation functional; no ECP |
| Quantum nuclei | both hydrogens in the new CNEO-DFT inputs (the April 2026 HX/HY runs had only the acceptor proton quantum) |
| Systems | HCN···HF, HCN···HCl, HCN···HBr (hydrogen-bonded, bent and halogen-bonded starts); (HF)₂, HF/HCl, HF/HBr, (HCl)₂, HCl/HBr, (HBr)₂ |
| Masses | CNEO-DFT: `mol.mass` (quantum protons 1.007276 u, classical nuclei isotope-averaged); conventional DFT: isotope-averaged atomic masses only |

Nine chemical systems; the tenth CNEO-DFT folder in the repository is the
dense-chlorine-grid variant of HCN···HCl, not a further system.

## 2. What was found in the original runs

The original CNEO-DFT logs (HCN sets February 2026, HX/HY sets April 2026)
and the nine conventional-DFT log sets were parsed, every optimised structure
classified as a minimum or saddle, and the harmonic frequencies re-derived.

- **One cause behind the spurious imaginary modes.** PySCF's analytic
  Hessian, NEO and conventional RKS alike for GGA and hybrid-GGA functionals,
  holds the DFT grid fixed. The exchange–correlation block then violates the
  translational sum rule Σ_B H_AB = 0 by a diagonal-block defect δ_A that
  grows with nuclear charge and basis size: ~1e-3 Eh/Bohr² on fluorine at
  grid level 3, 0.07 to 0.4 on chlorine, 1.4 to 18 on bromine. After
  projection of the rigid-body motions the displaced translations leak into
  the softest intermolecular modes, giving 67i to 350i cm⁻¹ bends in
  HCN···HCl, ~400i in HF/HCl, ~700i in HCl/HBr and 206i to 555i in (HBr)₂.
  Even the level-9 grid of the conventional-DFT runs left bromine defects of
  5e-3 to 2.7e-2 at aug-cc-pVTZ/QZ, enough for spurious 47i to 77i modes in
  the floppy (HBr)₂ complex.
- **Repair.** Replace each diagonal block by minus the sum of its row's
  off-diagonal blocks, then symmetrise. A 400-radial-shell HCN···HBr probe
  showed the defect is radial quadrature error and that the repaired
  spectrum matches the converged one to 0.1 cm⁻¹. Raw and repaired spectra
  are now printed side by side in every new driver.
- **Grid level.** The original CNEO-DFT runs sat at PySCF's default level 3
  although level 9 had been written into the cluster copy of `neo/ks.py`:
  the inputs set no grid, the patched lines sat behind the EPC-only early
  returns, and `mf.grids` on the NEO object is the EPC screening grid, not
  the electronic one (`GRID_LEVEL_NOTE.md`).
- **Other failure modes.** The pre-2.14 fork dropped one bending mode of
  linear complexes (3N−6 instead of 3N−5 frequencies); the CPHF Krylov
  solver died at its iteration cap on bent isomers; all aug-cc-pVTZ
  HCN···HBr Hessians, CNEO and DFT, died at one stage (memory or walltime);
  several local-minimum runs started from undefined geometries.

## 3. New input sets (committed)

Self-contained PySCF drivers starting from the original final geometries,
generated by a `make_inputs.py` per set, with SLURM arrays per basis tier
(`dz`/`tz`/`qz`), a README classifying the original runs, and the analysis
scripts and classification tables alongside.

| Folder | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `hcn_hf`, `hcn_hcl`, `hcn_hbr` | CNEO-DFT | linear hydrogen-bonded, bent and (Cl, Br) linear halogen-bonded starts |
| `hcn_hcl_densegrid` | CNEO-DFT | 400 radial shells on Cl, `cl400` file tag, `densegrid` cluster subdirectory |
| `hf_hf`, `hcl_hcl`, `hbr_hbr` | CNEO-DFT | Cs minimum plus C₂h saddle start; donor placed first |
| `hf_hbr`, `hf_hcl`, `hcl_hbr` | CNEO-DFT | both donor isomers, labelled by donor |
| `*_dft` (nine folders) | conventional DFT | same geometries, settings and tables as the CNEO sets; isotope-averaged masses |

Shared settings of every new driver:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| SCF | `conv_tol` 1e-11, `conv_tol_grad` 1e-6, 200 cycles |
| CPHF | `conv_tol_cpscf` 1e-9, 300 iterations, retry with level shift 0.2 |
| DFT grid | level 5 on all atoms plus `atom_grid` 400 × 770 on halogens (F in the HF sets and the dense variants, Cl, Br); grid response in the gradients |
| Optimizer | geomeTRIC, `GAU_TIGHT`, `convergence_energy` 1e-8, `subfrctor` 2, 200-step cap, stop if the optimisation fails |
| Analysis | translational sum-rule check and diagonal-block repair, grid report, key distances with dissociation warning, full 3N and rigid-body-projected frequency tables |

The HCN···HF and HCN···HCl plain CNEO-DFT sets predate the sum-rule repair
and grid report and still use the older template.

## 4. CNEO-DFT against conventional DFT

From the classification of the nine conventional-DFT log sets against the
CNEO-DFT ones at matching functional and basis:

- the lighter hydrogen halide is the preferred donor in the mixed dimers,
  by 0.6 to 1.1 kcal/mol (HF/HBr), 0.3 to 0.7 (HF/HCl) and 0.2 to 0.4
  (HCl/HBr);
- a quantum donor proton shortens the hydrogen bond by 0.03 to 0.09 Å and
  raises the intermolecular modes; a quantum acceptor proton changes them by
  less than 0.015 Å and 10 cm⁻¹;
- quantum protons lengthen the N···Cl and N···Br halogen bonds by 0.03 to
  0.07 Å;
- the X–H stretch is lowered by 80 to 270 cm⁻¹ when its proton is quantum.

## 5. Documents

- `METHODS.md` (committed, PDF delivered twice): Part A software, model,
  systems, quantum nuclei, settings of both generations of runs, the
  re-optimisation settings, the analysis protocol, the sum-rule violations
  found and the conventional-DFT reference set with per-system results and
  a cross-system summary; Part B a graduate-level derivation of the
  fixed-grid sum-rule defect, its effect after projection and the repair,
  typeset in LaTeX; Part C suggested methods wording and 21 references.
- `GRID_LEVEL_NOTE.md` (committed, PDF delivered): why the original
  CNEO-DFT runs stayed at grid level 3, with the patched code, the fork
  line references, the working setting and a grid-size table.
- This summary. All three documents, the description of every input set
  and the classification tables of the original runs are also collected in
  `operaghost5/dimer-systems`, grouped by kind, with PDF renderings.

## 6. Delivered but not committed

Four dense-halogen-grid sets for HCN···HF (`f400`) and HCN···HCl (`cl400`),
CNEO-DFT and conventional DFT, were delivered as
`hcn_hf_cneo_dft_inputs_densegrid.zip`, `hcn_hcl_cneo_dft_inputs_densegrid.zip`,
`hcn_hf_dft_inputs_densegrid.zip` and `hcn_hcl_dft_inputs_densegrid.zip`.
Their SLURM scripts read from and write to the usual cluster paths with an
extra `densegrid` subdirectory. Committing them would replace the existing
`hcn_hcl_densegrid` folder (the new version adds the grid report), so they
wait for your word.
